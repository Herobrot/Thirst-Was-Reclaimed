### Campfires and Furnaces
After harvesting water, to avoid [sickness](https://github.com/mlus-asuka/Thirst-Was-Reclaimed/wiki/Water-Purity#purity-related-effects), a player can purify water, in any vessel, both on a campfire or in a furnace. If boiled on a campfire, water will get a +1 purification level. Doing so at higher temperatures, in a furnace, will instead purify it by 2 levels.

Using a bucket is the most efficient way of purifying water, as it holds the equivalent of 3 bottles. However, since a player can't drink directly from a bucket, the water will need to be dumped in a cauldron and picked back up with bottles.

### Create Purification 
###### if you're not familiar with Create, you might wanna read [their wiki](https://github.com/Creators-of-Create/Create/wiki) before reading any further

![](https://imgur.com/CZqidfe.png)

If [Create](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/create) is installed, a new contraption will appear in the creative menu: a **Sand Filter**.

This block can receive water from a pipe pointing on the **top** side, which will be filtered out (a +1 purification level) and can be pumped out from the **bottom**. Keep in mind that since the sand filter counts as a tank, a **mechanical pump** is needed to extract the purified water from the bottom.
