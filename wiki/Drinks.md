### Vanilla Drinks
The 2 current drinks options for vanilla are **Terracotta Bowls** and **Water Bottles (which can now be stacked up to 16)**. 

If the player doesn't yet have access to a furnace, they can dig some clay from a river, use it for a clay bowl, which can then be fired and turned into a terracotta bowl, usable as a water container. The wooden bowl isn't usable since it would burn if placed in a campfire or a furnace.

### AppleSkin
If [AppleSkin](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/appleskin) is installed, a tooltip showing an item's hydration and "quenchness" value will show up in the inventory.

### Farmer's Delight & Addons
Currently all the drinks added by the mods **Farmer's Delight**, **Farmer's Respite** and **Brewin' and Chewin'** can be used to quench one's thirst. In fact, **teas** or **booze** are the best drink options for late game. Teas also include Create's beloved Builder's Tea

### Loot chests
Certain drinks from the mods mentioned above can also be found as loot in various structures, along with **Purified Water Bottles**:

- **Mead, Salty Folly and Black Tea** can be found in **Shipwrecks**
- **Beer, Apple Cider and Yellow Tea** in **Abandoned Mineshafts**
- **Vodka, Yellow Tea and Dandelion Tea** in **Dungeons**
- **Red Rum, Strongroot Ale and Rose Hip Tea** in **Nether Fortresses** and **Bastions**
