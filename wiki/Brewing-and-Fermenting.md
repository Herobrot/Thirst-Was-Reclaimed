If either [Farmer's Respite](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/farmers-respite) or [Brewin' and Chewin'](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/brewin-and-chewin) are installed, water purity will influence the results of brewing and fermenting recipes.

### Brewing
When using a **kettle**, the purity of the water inside it can be seen by hovering on the kettle gui's water level display. The output of a brewing recipe will be slightly more purified than the starting water (**+1 purification level**), to account for boiling.

### Fermenting
The water used in a **keg** for fermenting must be **purified** (in which case the output will be purified too), otherwise bacteria will proliferate through the fermentation process, making the output far worse to drink than the starting water (**-2 purification levels**).
